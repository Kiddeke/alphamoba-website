The kit

  • P

    The Canker

    Abilities infect the target with a Canker, dealing magic damage every second. Damage to the host from any other source fills a hidden meter. Filling the meter or killing the host bursts the Canker, damaging enemies nearby and giving each a weaker Canker. A Canker that expires does nothing. Any unit that dies carrying one restores mana.

  • Q

    Feed

    35 mana · 5s cooldown · range 5.75

    Fire a bolt of fel light for magic damage.

  • W

    Afflict

    70 mana · 9s cooldown · range 6.25

    Infect one enemy with a first-generation Canker.

  • E

    Gather

    60 mana · 18s cooldown · range 9

    Slow every cankered enemy nearby and pull cankered champions a step toward each other. You gain movement speed.

  • R

    Contagion

    100 mana · 140s cooldown · range 9

    Seed a wide area with fresh Cankers. For 10s your Cankers cost less to burst, burst 50% wider, and spread a second generation to champions.
