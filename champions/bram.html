The kit

  • P

    Hold the Line

    Damage from your front arc is reduced by 20%, plus 2% per enemy champion facing you, to a maximum of 3. Damage from behind lands in full and reveals the attacker to your team. Frontal mitigation is disabled while the Gate stands.

  • Q

    Gatecrash

    50 mana · 10s cooldown · range 3

    Slam the Gate forward, damaging enemies and knocking them back. Enemies knocked into terrain or into your Gate take bonus damage and are stunned.

  • W

    Raise the Gate

    70 mana · 18s cooldown · range 4

    Plant the Gate as a one-way wall for 4s. Allies pass through it; enemies and enemy dashes are blocked. Your frontal mitigation is disabled while it stands.

  • E

    Shoulder to Shoulder

    60 mana · 16s cooldown

    Brace for 2.5s. You cannot be displaced and are slowed by 20%. Allies behind you take 25% less damage from sources in front of you.

  • R

    Bastion

    100 mana · 140s cooldown · range 3.5

    Leap and slam the Gate down, raising a one-way rampart around the landing point for 6s. Enemies caught in the wall line are knocked inward and stunned.
