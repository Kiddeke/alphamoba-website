The kit

  • P

    Ground Covered

    Four seconds of uninterrupted travel grants bonus movement speed, lost when a champion damages you. Near your own Milestones you take less damage and attack faster.

  • Q

    Rod and Chain

    40 mana · 9s cooldown · range 3.5

    Sweep the rod in an arc. Hitting exactly one enemy deals bonus damage and refunds most of the cooldown.

  • W

    Set Stone

    60 mana · 30s cooldown · range 8.5

    Plant a Milestone at your feet, to a maximum of two. It grants vision to your team and hastens allies near it. Only champions and turrets can destroy it, and both teams can see it.

  • E

    Twenty-Two Yards

    55 mana · 20s cooldown · range 6.5

    Throw the chain to tether an enemy champion for 2.5s. Neither of you can move beyond the chain's length; whoever crosses it is pulled back, damaged and slowed.

  • R

    The Long Walk

    100 mana · 160s cooldown · range 8

    Channel for 4s, then travel to one of your Milestones and arrive with a shield. Hard crowd control, enough damage, or destroying the Milestone cancels it.
