The kit

  • P

    Forge Breath

    Heat runs 0 to 100 and refills 4 a second. Your attacks are gouts of flame that spend 8 Heat and splash 40% down a jet behind the target. Above 60 Heat the jet reaches 0.75 further and burns for 15% of your attack damage over 2s. Below 15 Heat it sputters for 30% less.

  • Q

    Gout

    15 heat · 7s cooldown · range 6

    A cone of fire that spends up to 40 Heat: physical damage, 1% more per Heat spent, and a burn.

  • W

    Pump

    4s cooldown

    Hold to work the bellows for up to 2s: +45 Heat a second, but you are slowed by 40% and cannot attack. Release (or press again) to stop. A full pump gives your next 3 attacks 30% attack speed.

  • E

    Backdraft

    20 heat · 16s cooldown

    Blast the nozzle at the ground and rocket away. Enemies where you took off take physical damage and are slowed by 30% for 1s.

  • R

    White Heat

    100 heat · 90s cooldown · range 9

    Needs 100 Heat. Swell for 0.75s, then a white-hot blast in a long cone: physical damage, a 3s burn and healing cut by 40% for 4s. Then you Overheat: Heat drops to 0 and cannot refill for 3s.
