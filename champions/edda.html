The kit

  • P

    Keeper's Eye

    Your attack range grows by 0.25 at levels 3, 6, 9, 12 and 15.

  • Q

    Flare Round

    30 mana · 7s cooldown

    Your next shot deals bonus damage, reveals its target for 4s, and resets your attack timer. Attacking a revealed target grants stacking attack speed.

  • W

    Storm Lantern

    50 mana · 24s cooldown · range 11

    Throw a lantern that stands for 45s, granting vision around it and slowing enemies in its light. Two may stand at once, and two hits destroy one.

  • E

    Not Tonight

    40 mana · 16s cooldown · range 5

    Brace for 2s. The first enemy to dash at you is slowed heavily and you step back. If none does, most of the cost is refunded.

  • R

    The Dawnwatch

    100 mana · 120s cooldown · range 9

    Light the great lamp. The beam sweeps a full circle over 4s, damaging, slowing and revealing everything it passes. You may walk slowly while it turns.
