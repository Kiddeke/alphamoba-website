The kit

  • P

    Firn

    Your ice is solid to enemies and passable to allies, who cross it faster, and it blocks projectiles from both teams. Near your own ice you take 15% less damage.

  • Q

    Icecutter

    45 mana · 8s cooldown · range 7

    Send a crack racing down a line for magic damage and a slow.

  • W

    Standing Floe

    60 mana · 13s cooldown · range 7

    Raise a pillar of ice: 0.5s to rise, 8s standing, four champion hits to break. Recast on it to shatter it for damage and a shove of shards.

  • E

    Black Ice

    50 mana · 16s cooldown · range 8

    Freeze a strip of ground for 8s. Allies cross it faster, enemies slower, and you fastest.

  • R

    The Slow Winter

    100 mana · 140s cooldown · range 12

    Raise a wall of ice across the field. It rises from the middle outward, stands 5s, blocks enemies and every projectile, and allies pass through it. Recast to shatter it along its length.
