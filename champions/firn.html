The kit

  • P

    Firn

    Your ice is solid to enemies and passable to allies, who cross it faster, and it blocks projectiles from both teams. Near your own ice you take 15% less damage.

  • Q

    Icecutter

    45 mana · 8s cooldown · range 7

    Send a crack racing down a line for magic damage and a slow.

  • W

    Calving Shelf

    60 mana · 13s cooldown · range 7

    Aim a point within 7m. After 0.5s a shelf of ice grows out of the nearest wall, or the nearest of your own ice, within 4m of that point, extending 3m toward it in three segments that stand 6s: solid to enemies, open to allies, four champion hits each to break. With nothing to grow from, a two-segment stub rises at the point. Recast on the shelf to shatter all of it for 70/105/140/175/210 (+40% AP) magic damage and a 1m shove each, at half the cooldown and no mana. Two shelves at most.

  • E

    Black Ice

    50 mana · 16s cooldown · range 8

    Freeze a strip of ground for 8s. Allies cross it faster, enemies slower, and you fastest.

  • R

    The Slow Winter

    100 mana · 140s cooldown · range 7.5

    Raise a 6m-wide front of ice at your feet that creeps forward along your aim at 1.5m a second for 5s. Enemies it meets are pushed ahead of it (pinned 0.75s if pressed into a wall); it blocks enemy shots and movement, and allies walk through it. Recast, or let its time run out, and the whole front shatters for 200/300/400 (+60% AP) magic damage and a 1.5m shove.
