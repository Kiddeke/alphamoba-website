The kit

  • P

    The Open Tap

    You have no mana. Abilities cost current health and cannot be cast if the cost would take you below 15% of maximum. Health spent becomes Owed, which your curses repay. Enemies carrying one of your curses deal 10% less damage to you. Out of combat you regenerate quickly.

  • Q

    Utterance

    6s cooldown · range 6

    Send one syllable down a line, stopping on the first enemy. Hitting the host of the Word restarts it. A kill refunds the health it cost.

  • W

    The Word

    11s cooldown · range 6.25

    Curse one champion for 7s with damage that grows every second; the last tick deals more than twice the first. The health you paid becomes its Debt: letting it run repays you with interest, and a host who dies repays half and passes the curse to the nearest champion. Only one may exist.

  • E

    Second Hand

    18s cooldown

    The Word's host convulses, applying a weaker, briefer Echo to every other champion near them. Echoes repay nothing. If no one is in range it does not cast and costs nothing.

  • R

    Say It All The Way Through

    140s cooldown · range 7

    Apply a Deep Word for 10s. It stacks with the Word, cannot be cleansed, and passes to a new host each time its host dies.
