The kit

  • P

    The Little Ghost

    Every 12s the next allied champion her abilities touch keeps her ghost wisp for 4s. The first champion to hit them loses 40 (+10 per level) (+15% AP) of that damage to the wisp and is spooked, slowed 30% for 1s.

  • Q

    Spirit Round

    55 mana · 9s cooldown · range 10.5

    Fire a ghost round down a line. It passes through allied champions, shielding each for 2s, and stops on the first enemy: magic damage and a 25% slow for 1.25s.

  • W

    Haunt

    70 mana · 14s cooldown · range 6.5

    Send the little ghost at an enemy champion. It is Haunted for 4s, taking magic damage over the duration; the first ability from one of her allies to hit it pops the Haunt for the same again and it flees in terror from Hana.

  • E

    Ghost Step

    50 mana · 18s cooldown

    Half-step into the spirit world for 2s: walk through units with 35% movement speed that fades over the duration, and cut 3s off the next Spirit Round.

  • R

    Séance

    100 mana · 150s cooldown

    Open a séance around Hana for 8s. Every allied champion in it is shielded for 3s; while it holds, an ally in it who would die rises as a Ghost for 4s at 30% health, draining to nothing, unhealable, fighting at full strength, then dies to the original killer.
