The kit

  • P

    The Kiln Never Goes Out

    The kiln stokes a charge of Clay every 12s and holds three; the count is visible to both teams. A clayfolk that dies shatters for magic damage and leaves bisque that slows.

  • Q

    Throwing Clay

    50 mana · 8s cooldown · range 7

    Hurl a lump of clay for damage and a slow where it lands. Landing it on one of your clayfolk repairs it instead and doubles its next swing.

  • W

    Fire a Clayfolk

    30 mana · 1s cooldown · range 6.5

    Spend a Clay to fire a clayfolk at a nearby point. It attacks the nearest enemy champion within 6, and walks to you and waits if there is none. It blocks enemy champion movement.

  • E

    Handoff

    40 mana · 18s cooldown · range 9

    Swap places with one of your clayfolk. The one arriving where you stood gains movement speed briefly.

  • R

    The Great Firing

    100 mana · 120s cooldown · range 8

    Glaze every living clayfolk for 12s: more health, larger, faster-swinging and harder to break. You refill to three Clay, and if fewer than two clayfolk stand, two are fired at your feet first.
