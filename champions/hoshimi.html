The kit

  • P

    Star-Catcher

    Every 15s (down to 9s at level 18) a star falls near her and lies there for 8s. An allied champion who walks over it heals 30 (+10 per level) and gains 20% movement speed for 2s; if Hoshimi catches it she bottles it in a jar instead (up to 2). An enemy who walks over it stamps it out.

  • Q

    Shooting Star

    50 mana · 7s cooldown · range 9.5

    Flick a star down a line. The first enemy hit takes magic damage, and the star bounces to the nearest allied champion near it, healing them.

  • W

    Hang a Star

    60 mana · 9s cooldown · range 7.5

    Hang a star over an allied champion or herself for 5s, healing every second. If they drop below 40% health from champion damage it falls at once and heals the rest in one go, 30% stronger. Two may hang at a time; a jar makes it free and 50% stronger.

  • E

    Star Net

    70 mana · 14s cooldown · range 8.5

    Throw the star net down a line. The first enemy champion is caught: rooted and dealt magic damage. Minions and monsters block it.

  • R

    Falling Star

    100 mana · 120s cooldown · range 14

    Call a star down on a point. After 1.25s it lands: allies are healed; enemies take magic damage and are blinded. A catchable star rests in the crater for 4s.
