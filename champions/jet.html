The kit

  • P

    Late Stroke

    Everything you land leaves a Stroke. Three seconds later it falls, dealing its damage and healing you for most of it. Dying voids every outstanding Stroke. No one else can heal or shield you.

  • Q

    Backswing

    35 mana · 8s cooldown · range 3.2

    Cut through the arc in front of you. Recast for the return swing back across it.

  • W

    Stay

    50 mana · 20s cooldown · range 8.5

    For 2.5s most damage you take is deferred into a pool instead of landing, then arrives over the following 3s. Strokes that fall in the meantime pay the pool down first.

  • E

    Downstroke

    45 mana · 14s cooldown

    Drive the point in. Every Stroke resting on a champion nearby falls at once at reduced value, slowing whoever loses one.

  • R

    On Time

    100 mana · 120s cooldown · range 8

    For a few seconds Strokes land the instant they are applied and heal for more, and every outstanding Stroke falls on cast. Afterwards new Strokes take 5s to fall instead of 3.
