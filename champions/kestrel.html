The kit

  • P

    The Stoop

    Your basic attacks are lure throws. Every 4th attack on the same champion, the kestrel stoops on them for 40% of your attack damage + 4% of their missing health as bonus physical damage, from overhead, so nothing that blocks shots stops it. Every 2nd attack on your Quarry. No stoops while the bird is away on Cast Off.

  • Q

    Lure Cast

    50 mana · 9s cooldown · range 8

    Swing the lure down a line. The first enemy hit takes physical damage and becomes Quarry for 6s: revealed to your team, and stooped on every 2nd attack. The kestrel stoops on it at once.

  • W

    Cast Off

    60 mana · 18s cooldown · range 11

    Send the kestrel to circle a point for 6s, giving sight around it (both teams see the bird). The first enemy champion in its circle is stooped on, slowed by 35% for 1.5s and made Quarry. Recast to call it home. No passive stoops while it is away.

  • E

    Bate

    40 mana · 16s cooldown

    Hop away in a fury of wings. Enemies close to where you took off are blinded for 1s (longer with rank): their basic attacks miss.

  • R

    Out of Sight

    100 mana · 110s cooldown · range 14

    The kestrel climbs out of sight. After 1.5s it stoops on the lowest-health enemy champion near you (Quarry first), then twice more 1s apart, choosing again each time. Each stoop is marked by a ring 0.75s before it lands and hits whoever is in the ring for physical damage + 12% of their missing health. You can keep attacking.
