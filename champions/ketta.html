Ketta

The One Spear

FighterNo resource

The kit

  • P

    One Spear

    Ketta has one trident. Planted, it is a visible object anyone can stand on; while it is planted her attacks are fists at 125 range for 50% damage and she cannot cast Q. Walking onto it picks it up. Spear hits on champions add a Notch (max 5) that the throw spends: +8% damage and +0.1s pin each.

  • Q

    Thrown Spear

    12s cooldown · range 8

    Throw the trident 800 units, stopping on the first enemy champion: physical damage and a pin (0.75s +0.15s per rank) where they stand. The spear plants at their feet, or at the end of the line if it hits nothing.

  • W

    Run Down

    16s cooldown

    Gain 30% move speed for 2.5s. If the spear is planted the speed is doubled toward it, and reaching it during the buff refunds half of W's cooldown.

  • E

    Call It Back

    18s cooldown · range 9

    The planted trident tears free and flies back to her hand from up to 900 units, hitting everyone on the line for physical damage and knocking them aside; a champion still pinned by it takes 40% more.

  • R

    The Long Throw

    110s cooldown · range 14

    After a 0.75s wind-up, hurl the trident in an arc up to 1400 units. It plants where it lands and pins every enemy champion within 250 for 1.5s with physical damage, doubled on the champion it lands on. For 8s the planted spear is a marker both teams see through fog, and W toward it is tripled.
