The kit

  • P

    Two Places at Once

    While your fox-double stands, every Q and W you cast is cast again by the double, from where it stands and at the same point, for 50% damage. An enemy hit by both the spell and its echo is Bound: pinned for 0.5s (once per 6s each).

  • Q

    Foxfire Talisman

    50 mana · 6s cooldown · range 9

    A talisman bursts on the first enemy it meets: magic damage and a 25% slow for 1.5s.

  • W

    Binding Circle

    70 mana · 13s cooldown · range 7.5

    Lay a ring of talismans. After 0.75s it closes: magic damage, and enemies inside are pinned for 1s.

  • E

    Fox-Double

    60 mana · 18s cooldown

    Dash and leave a foxfire double where you stood for 10s. Enemies see you until a champion hits it; two champion hits break it. Recast to swap places with it.

  • R

    Nine Tails

    100 mana · 110s cooldown · range 8

    Trade places with your double (placing one first if none stands), then both loose 5 wisps over 2s at the nearest enemies within 6, each for magic damage. A champion hit by wisps from both takes 30% more from every later wisp.
