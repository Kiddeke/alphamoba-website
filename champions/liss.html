The kit

  • P

    Tied Off

    Overhealing becomes a shield worth 40% of the excess, to a maximum of 8% of the target's health, for 3s. Stitches that expire on their own refund 30 mana.

  • Q

    Needle Dart

    60 mana · 9s cooldown · range 9

    Throw the needle in a piercing line, damaging enemies and healing allies, then it returns to you and hits everything a second time.

  • W

    Stitch

    70 mana · 14s cooldown · range 7

    Sew a thread between two nearby units, to a maximum of two threads. Threaded allies echo half of your heals to each other and split damage; threaded enemies share 25% of everything either takes. Recast on a thread to Snip it, healing allies or burning enemies.

  • E

    Basting Pins

    65 mana · 13s cooldown

    Scatter pins in an area, damaging and slowing enemies inside. The first to leave is rooted briefly.

  • R

    The Grand Seam

    100 mana · 130s cooldown · range 12

    Sew a seam across the battlefield for 5s. Allies crossing it are healed and hastened once; enemies crossing take damage and are slowed, shared by every Stitched enemy.
