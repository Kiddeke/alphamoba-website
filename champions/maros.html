The kit

  • P

    Undertow

    Attacks and abilities apply a stack of Brine, to a maximum of 3. At 3 stacks the target is slowed 30% for 1.5s and Chain Sweep fouls them twice as long.

  • Q

    Chain Sweep

    70 mana · 12s cooldown · range 10

    Haul the chain taut between you and your Mooring (up to 10m of it). Every enemy on it takes 70/110/150/190/230 (+60% AP) magic damage and is fouled: pinned for 0.6s, or 1.2s at 3 Brine. With no Mooring, lash the chain 6m straight ahead instead.

  • W

    Breakwall

    60 mana · 12s cooldown · range 14

    Plant or relocate the Mooring within 14m, visible to both teams and sunk by 3 enemy hits. Where it lands, its chain lies 6m back toward you as a boom for 2s: enemies cannot walk or dash over it; allies and projectiles pass. Replanting lays a new chain.

  • E

    Breakwater

    50 mana · 11s cooldown

    Sweep the chain around you, damaging enemies and knocking them away from the Mooring, or away from you if none stands.

  • R

    Weigh Anchor

    100 mana · 120s cooldown · range 10

    Haul the Mooring home: its path is drawn, then over 1s the anchor drags along the ground to your feet. Every enemy champion within 1.5m of its path is snagged and dragged along with it, arriving stunned for 1s and taking 180/270/360 (+70% AP) magic damage (20% more if Brined). With no Mooring down, you first hurl the anchor 10m ahead, then haul. The Mooring then sits at your feet.
