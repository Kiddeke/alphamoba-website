The kit

  • P

    Undertow

    Attacks and abilities apply a stack of Brine, to a maximum of 3. At 3 stacks the target is slowed 30% for 1.5s and your next hook against them travels faster.

  • Q

    Anchor Cast

    70 mana · 14s cooldown · range 10.5

    Hurl the anchor in a line. The first enemy hit is dragged in an arc toward your Mooring, or toward you if none stands.

  • W

    Drop Mooring

    60 mana · 12s cooldown · range 14

    Plant or relocate the Mooring, visible to both teams and destroyed by 3 enemy hits. Allies who right-click it dash to it and gain a shield.

  • E

    Breakwater

    50 mana · 11s cooldown

    Sweep the chain around you, damaging enemies and knocking them away from the Mooring, or away from you if none stands.

  • R

    The Harbor Calls

    100 mana · 140s cooldown · range 8

    Channel 1s, then chains erupt from the Mooring and latch every enemy champion near it, slowing them 40%. After 2s, all still in the radius are dragged to the Mooring, damaged and stunned.
