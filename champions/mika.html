The kit

  • P

    Rivet Line

    Every 4th basic attack fires a rivet that punches straight through: it hits the target and everything in a 5 line behind it for 70% damage, and Tags enemy champions for 6s (revealed to your team).

  • Q

    Drum Dump

    40 mana · 12s cooldown

    Empty the drum: 70% attack speed for 2.5s, and each attack counts twice toward Rivet Line.

  • W

    Spotting Flare

    50 mana · 16s cooldown · range 11

    Fire a flare that lights a circle for 5s for your team. After 0.6s it bursts: enemies inside take physical damage and are Tagged.

  • E

    Boot Jets

    50 mana · 15s cooldown

    Hop on steam jets. Your next basic attack within 3s is a Rivet Line.

  • R

    Walker Strike

    100 mana · 120s cooldown · range 20

    Mark a line that starts up to 6 out toward the cursor. After 1s the walker strides along it: five footfalls 4 apart, 0.4s between, each stamping its drawn circle for physical damage and a 0.5s knock-up. A footfall near a Tagged champion lands on them. Later footfalls deal half to anyone already stamped.
