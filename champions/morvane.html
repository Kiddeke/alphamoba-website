The kit

  • P

    Paid in Full

    The candle burns down in combat and relights out of it. The more of it is lit, the more damage you deal and the less you take. Landing an ability on an enemy champion restores candle.

  • Q

    Doorbreaker

    15 candle · 9s cooldown · range 3.5

    Cleave a wide arc in front of you. Hitting an enemy champion refunds half the cooldown and restores candle; minions and monsters refund nothing.

  • W

    I Am Still Coming

    20 candle · 24s cooldown · range 8.5

    For 2.5s you are immune to slows, crowd control on you is 60% shorter, you walk through units, and you gain 25% movement speed.

  • E

    The Toll

    15 candle · 13s cooldown · range 3

    Strike one target for weapon damage plus 6% of their maximum health, capped against non-champions. Enemies below 8% health are executed, and a kill relights the candle in full.

  • R

    The Collection

    25 candle · 120s cooldown · range 18

    Channel, then march unstoppably to the chosen point, cleaving everything on the path. The path is drawn for both teams before you move.
