The kit

  • P

    Drawing a Crowd

    Trading blows with enemy champions builds Hype on a 0-100 meter both teams can read. Dealing and taking hits both earn it, and it decays out of combat. At 100 Hype you gain movement speed toward enemy champions until you spend it.

  • Q

    One-Two

    30 mana · 7s cooldown · range 1.75

    Your next basic attack within 3s strikes twice, and you gain movement speed toward enemy champions briefly.

  • W

    Haymaker

    40 mana · 14s cooldown · range 4

    Spend all Hype to guard for 1.25s behind a shield that scales with the Hype spent, with the cone drawn on the ground. Then throw the Haymaker for heavy damage plus a share of everything the guard absorbed.

  • E

    Cut the Ring

    55 mana · 16s cooldown · range 4.5

    Pull everything caught to either side of you inward. Enemies caught on both sides are slammed together in front of you and stunned; a catch on one side only is pulled and slowed.

  • R

    The Main Event

    100 mana · 110s cooldown · range 3.75

    Grab an enemy champion, carry them, and slam them down for heavy damage, splashing and slowing enemies near the landing. The slam drops the Ring: for a few seconds you earn double Hype inside it, and enemies leaving it are briefly slowed.
