The kit

  • P

    Light Fingers

    Every third hit (attacks or abilities) on an enemy champion picks their pocket, once per champion every 12s: steal 4 gold (+1 per 3 levels) and roll Loot for 4s: 20% movement speed, 25% attack speed, or a 40 (+8 per level) shield.

  • Q

    Hook and Swipe

    40 energy · 5s cooldown · range 2.5

    Rake both hooks through a 2.5 m cone for 45/75/105/135/165 (+0.8 bonus AD) physical damage. Champions hit count twice toward Light Fingers.

  • W

    Rooftop Run

    50 energy · 14s cooldown · range 5

    Hook an enemy or terrain within 5 m and haul yourself to it. On terrain, vault to the far side of a wall up to 2.5 m thick; on an enemy champion, land beside them and slow them 30% for 1s.

  • E

    Muffle the Bell

    60 energy · 22s cooldown

    Become invisible for 3s and gain 15% movement speed. An enemy champion within 3 m reveals you; attacking or casting ends it. Your first attack out of it picks a pocket at once, ignoring the 12s lockout, and slows 30% for 1s.

  • R

    The Heist

    100s cooldown · range 7

    Mark a 4.5 m ring at a point within 7 m and sprint its circumference in 1s, untargetable, dealing 120/180/240 (+0.7 bonus AD) physical damage to everyone you pass and picking every champion's pocket regardless of lockout. Enemies inside the ring are slowed 40% while you run. You end where you started.
