The kit

  • P

    Swept Clean

    Any ally you move with Shoo! (including yourself) is shaken free of slows and shielded for 50 (+12 per level) (+0.3 AP) for 2.5s.

  • Q

    Sweep

    50 mana · 9s cooldown · range 3.5

    Sweep the broom in a 3.5m arc in front of you: 60/95/130/165/200 (+0.5 AP) magic damage, and enemies are swept 2m away from you.

  • W

    Shoo!

    60 mana · 20s cooldown · range 7

    Target an ally champion within 7m (or none, for yourself) and aim: a gust carries them 4m that way, through units. It moves stunned and pinned allies too (it does not end the stun). Triggers Swept Clean.

  • E

    Broomstick

    50 mana · 14s cooldown · range 5

    Ride the broom 5m in the aimed direction, passing through units. For 3s your path stays a trail of dry leaves where allies move 20% faster and enemies 15% slower.

  • R

    Salt the Step

    100 mana · 130s cooldown · range 6

    Sweep a huge 6m, 70-degree cone: enemies inside take 120/200/280 (+0.6 AP) magic damage and are swept back to the cone's edge (up to 4m). A line of salt and herbs is laid along that edge for 3/3.5/4s. Enemies cannot cross it, walking or dashing; allies and every projectile pass freely.
