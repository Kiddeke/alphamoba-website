The kit

  • P

    Rich Soil

    Any unit that dies near you leaves a Seed for 6s. Your abilities bloom the Seeds they touch, healing you and dealing a small burst of magic damage.

  • Q

    Thornlash

    45 mana · 5s cooldown · range 5

    A vine whip lashes out in a line, damaging and slowing everything it touches. Lashing through your garden or a Seed deals more damage and doubles the slow.

  • W

    The Garden

    70 mana · 14s cooldown · range 6

    Sow a circle of carnivorous growth for 5s. Enemies inside are damaged every second and slowed, and you regenerate 2% of your health per second while standing in it. One garden at a time.

  • E

    Grasping Root

    60 mana · 12s cooldown · range 7.5

    Roots burst from the ground after a moment, damaging and rooting everyone above them. Cast inside your garden they rise twice as fast and hold 50% longer.

  • R

    Overgrowth

    100 mana · 100s cooldown · range 8

    Root yourself for 4s. You gain heavy damage reduction, your garden doubles in size and holds, and every second you pulse Thornlash damage around you and bloom every Seed in reach. You cannot move or be moved. Recast to end it.
