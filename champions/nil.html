The kit

  • P

    Born Empty

    You use no resource, gain nothing from ability power, and cannot be revealed by any effect other than ordinary sight. Your next attack on an enemy champion who cast an ability in the last 3s deals bonus damage.

  • Q

    Cudgel

    8s cooldown · range 3

    A blunt swing of the three-section staff, knocking back everyone it catches.

  • W

    Unmake

    16s cooldown · range 4

    Thrust the spear in a line. The first enemy champion hit has their active magic erased: shields stripped, buffs cleared, channels broken. Any planted object in the line is destroyed.

  • E

    The Short Chain

    18s cooldown · range 6

    Throw the chain. The first champion hit is bound for 1.5s: they keep their feet and may move, but crossing the leash snaps them back. A blink or dash across it breaks the chain.

  • R

    Name on the List

    120s cooldown · range 6

    Name one enemy champion, visible to both teams. For the duration their magic cannot damage or affect you, while their attacks and crowd control are unchanged. Their teammates are unaffected.
