MageMana

The kit

  • P

    Inkblot

    Your abilities stain enemies with Ink for 4s, stacking to 3. At 3 the ink fills their eyes: blinded (attacks miss) for 1.25s, and the stacks wash off. Once every 8s per target.

  • Q

    Ink Jet

    40 mana · 6s cooldown · range 9

    Squirt a jet of ink down a line, piercing: magic damage and 1 Ink to everything hit.

  • W

    Reef Grasp

    70 mana · 10s cooldown · range 8

    Tentacles burst up in a circle after 0.6s: magic damage, enemies dragged 1 toward the centre and slowed by 35% for 1.5s, and 1 Ink.

  • E

    Jet Away

    60 mana · 16s cooldown

    Blast away from the cursor, leaving an ink cloud where you stood for 3s. Enemies inside are slowed by 20% and gain 1 Ink each second.

  • R

    Ink Tide

    100 mana · 130s cooldown · range 9

    Flood a wide circle for 6s. Allied champions inside, you included, are hidden from enemies unless an enemy champion comes within 2.5 of them. Enemies inside take magic damage and gain 1 Ink each second.
