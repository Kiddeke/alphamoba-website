Oboro

FighterMalice

The kit

  • P

    Needle Grin

    Every third basic attack on the same target tears a Stitch: bonus magic damage, and Oboro heals for 45% of the hit. A Stitched target stays Stitched for 4s. Hitting champions builds Malice; it drains out of combat.

  • Q

    Unravel

    10s cooldown · range 7

    A lash of ink in a line. Minions and monsters it crosses are dragged toward Oboro and hurt; the first champion it reaches is hurt and pinned for 0.5s.

  • W

    Come Apart

    14s cooldown

    Oboro sheds a seam: a shield now, plus 1.2 per Malice spent, and the seam's own worth bleeds back out of him over 4s. The bleed cannot kill him.

  • E

    Skitter

    12s cooldown · range 8

    A short dash. Aimed at a Stitched champion it reaches almost twice as far, lands beside them and bites.

  • R

    Eat the Seam

    100s cooldown · range 5

    Oboro grabs a champion, swaps places with them and hurts them. For 4s every basic attack on them tears a Stitch.
