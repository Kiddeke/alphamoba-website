Old Ren

The Dragon in a Child's Shape

AssassinNo resource

The kit

  • P

    Older Than He Looks

    The first time Old Ren damages an enemy champion he hasn't hit in the last 10s, the hit deals bonus magic damage equal to 5% of their max health and Frightens them for 0.5s.

  • Q

    Fang Cleave

    7s cooldown

    Chop a 120-degree, 3 m arc for 75/110/145/180/215 (+0.9 bonus AD) physical damage. Frightened enemies take 50% more, and hitting one refunds half the cooldown.

  • W

    Magma Arms

    14s cooldown

    For 4s gain 20% attack speed; basic attacks splash 1.5 m around the target for 40% of their damage as magic damage, and heal you for 20% of the damage they deal to champions.

  • E

    Ember Wings

    15s cooldown

    Glide 5 m, over walls, leaving a burning trail for 3s that deals 20 (+0.1 bonus AD) magic damage per second and slows 20%. Landing within 3 m of an enemy champion makes your next attack within 2s leap to its target.

  • R

    True Shape

    100s cooldown · range 14

    Spring up on your wings: for 1s a dragon shadow sweeps a 14 m by 5 m path, dealing 150/250/350 (+0.8 bonus AD) magic damage and Frightening enemies for 1/1.25/1.5s. Land at the aimed point (up to 10 m, over walls); Fang Cleave is refreshed and everyone hit counts as fresh again.
