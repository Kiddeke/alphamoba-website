The kit

  • P

    Forge-Hot

    Each basic attack adds 12 Heat and each ability that hits adds 20 (max 100, seen by both teams). It cools 10 per second after 3s out of combat. Above 50 Heat your attacks deal +15 (+1.5 per level) magic damage. At 100 your next Strike While Hot Brands.

  • Q

    Strike While Hot

    9s cooldown · range 3

    Slam the anvil 3m ahead into a 2.25m circle: 70/110/150/190/230 (+0.6 AD) physical damage and a 35% slow for 1.5s. At 100 Heat it spends it all, stuns for 0.75s and Brands everyone hit for 5s: the next hit from your side on a Branded enemy deals +5% of their max health as magic damage.

  • W

    Work the Iron

    15s cooldown

    For 3s you hammer in rhythm: +40% attack speed, attacks add double Heat, and you take 15% less damage.

  • E

    Anvil Drop

    16s cooldown · range 6

    Leap to a point within 6m and slam down: enemies within 2.25m are knocked up for 0.75s and take 60/95/130/165/200 (+0.5 AD) physical damage (+20 Heat per champion hit).

  • R

    Quench

    120s cooldown

    Plunge the hammer into the ground: a scalding steam cloud bursts 5m round you for 4s, spending all Heat. Enemies inside take 30/45/60 magic damage per second (+1% per Heat spent) and are slowed 25%. Enemies outside cannot see in: it hides your team like brush. You gain 30/40/50 armour and magic resist for the duration.
