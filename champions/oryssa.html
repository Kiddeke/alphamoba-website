The kit

  • P

    Wyrmblood

    Basic attacks build 8 Fury, abilities 10, and taking champion damage 3. Above 50 Fury you gain 10% damage reduction. Fury decays out of combat, and at 100 it can be spent on Drake Form.

  • Q

    Talon Rake

    8s cooldown

    Your next basic attack strikes twice and resets your attack timer. In Drake Form both strikes cleave.

  • W

    Cinderwake

    12s cooldown · range 8.5

    Burn nearby enemies for 3s and gain 20% movement speed for the duration. Drake Form widens the radius.

  • E

    Wyrmfire Spit

    10s cooldown

    Spit a fireball at the first enemy hit, marking them for 4s. Your attacks on a marked target deal 3% of their maximum health as bonus damage.

  • R

    LET IT OUT

    12s cooldown · range 8

    Requires 100 Fury. Leap to an area, damaging and knocking back enemies, then remain in Drake Form until your Fury empties: +200 health, longer cleaving attacks, and more damage reduction. Recast in Drake Form to spew a torrent of wyrmfire in a cone for massive damage, spending every drop of Fury and ending Drake Form. Dying in Drake Form grants the enemy team Wyrmslayer's Bounty.
