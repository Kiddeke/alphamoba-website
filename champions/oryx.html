The kit

  • P

    Star Chart

    Fall of a Star leaves a Star on the ground for 12s (up to 3). Allied champions near a Star are linked to it; the first time an ally is linked, and once per 10s after, they gain a shield of 30 (+8 per level) for 2s.

  • Q

    Fall of a Star

    55 mana · 7s cooldown · range 9

    Drop a star on a point. After 0.5s it lands for magic damage and a 30% slow for 1s, and stays as a Star.

  • W

    Draw the Line

    70 mana · 12s cooldown · range 9

    Draw a line from every Star to an allied champion or himself. The ally is shielded for 3s, 25% more per Star drawn; for those 3s an enemy champion touching a line takes magic damage, once.

  • E

    Antler Step

    60 mana · 16s cooldown · range 12

    Stride through the void to one of his Stars, arriving 0.25s later and consuming it; enemies at the arrival are knocked up. With no Star in reach, a short stride toward the cursor.

  • R

    The Constellation

    100 mana · 140s cooldown

    Place a Star at his feet, then for 4s join every Star and every allied champion near him into one web of lines. An enemy champion who crosses a line is rooted, takes magic damage and is revealed for the rest of the cast, once per cast.
