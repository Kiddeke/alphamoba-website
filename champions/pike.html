The kit

  • P

    Surplus

    You can only apply Bleed to champions whose maximum health is above your own, and each stack scales with the difference. Against anyone at or below your health you cannot Bleed and deal 15% less damage.

  • Q

    Fleam

    35 mana · 7s cooldown · range 3.5

    Thrust the lancet down a short line, piercing everything it meets, for two stacks of Bleed and bonus damage to minions.

  • W

    Tourniquet

    50 mana · 20s cooldown · range 8.5

    For 4s, bleeding champions have their healing and shielding cut heavily and their stacks stop decaying, and you gain attack speed for each one near you.

  • E

    Barb

    45 mana · 16s cooldown · range 4.5

    Drive the barb into a bleeding enemy and pull yourself to them, damaging and crippling them. It cannot be cast on anyone you cannot Bleed. You also gain movement speed toward the largest health pool in sight.

  • R

    Bleed Them White

    100 mana · 120s cooldown · range 5.5

    Lock onto one bleeding champion for 5s. Their Bleed is pinned at five stacks and doubled, and you take far less damage from everyone else. Killing them refunds half the cooldown.
