The kit

  • P

    Deadening

    Every disable you land leaves a Deadened mark for 8s, to a maximum of three. Each mark cuts the duration of your next disables on that target by a quarter. Disabling a target with no marks refunds mana and shortens the cooldown.

  • Q

    Dull Note

    45 mana · 7s cooldown · range 9

    One flat tone down a line, piercing everything it meets, for damage and a brief slow, with bonus damage to minions.

  • W

    The Felt

    70 mana · 20s cooldown · range 7

    After a short wind-up, a damping field settles on the ground. Enemy champions inside take damage and cannot cast, and may walk out at full speed.

  • E

    Dead Key

    60 mana · 16s cooldown · range 6.5

    Mark a champion for 1.5s. If they cast while it holds, the spell is lost on full cooldown and they are damaged and stunned. If they do not, half the cooldown is refunded.

  • R

    Dead Air

    100 mana · 140s cooldown · range 8

    Open a field on the ground, in range, in which no enemy champion may cast. While it holds you are slowed and cannot cast either, and it ends when you die. It deals no damage.
