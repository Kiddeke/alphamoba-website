The kit

  • P

    Tally

    Every 4th consecutive basic attack against the same target strikes twice, the second strike for 50% AD, and etches a permanent tally mark worth 1% attack speed. Switching targets resets the count but never the marks.

  • Q

    Hundred Cuts

    14s cooldown

    Blink between up to 4 nearby enemies, striking each. You are invisible and untargetable during the flurry, and emerge beside the first enemy cut; click mid-flurry to choose which side. Deals bonus damage to your Duel target. Basic attacks reduce this cooldown by 1s.

  • W

    Still Water

    16s cooldown · range 8.5

    Parry for 1.5s while rooted. The first champion hit against you is negated, granting 40% attack speed for 3s, and your next basic attack in that window deals bonus damage and slows by 30%.

  • E

    Keen Edge

    16s cooldown

    For 5s, gain 25% attack speed and deal bonus damage on every basic attack.

  • R

    The Hundredth Duel

    60s cooldown · range 8

    Duel an enemy champion for 8s. You gain 35% movement speed for the duration, and 35% attack speed and slow immunity while near them. Killing them during the duel resets all your cooldowns and grants +1 attack damage and 5 tally marks permanently. If they survive, the cooldown is increased by 20%.
