The kit

  • P

    Hoarfrost

    Every hit you land or take lays a layer of Hoar: on the enemy a stacking slow, on you stacking damage reduction, five layers each. An enemy at five is Brittle, and your next ability shatters them for bonus damage. Your own five call Hoarfall.

  • Q

    Splinter

    30 mana · 7s cooldown · range 5

    Flick the frost off your blade: an instant 5 m line of ice shards that pierces, dealing 60 (+0.7 bonus AD) physical damage and laying 1 Hoar on each enemy hit. If it shatters a Brittle enemy, splinters burst from them and lay 2 Hoar on every other enemy within 2.5 m.

  • W

    Frostbrand

    55 mana · 12s cooldown · range 2.4

    Plant the sword. Enemies nearby are dragged a step toward the blade and held as the ground rimes over under them.

  • E

    Cold Snap

    50 mana · 15s cooldown · range 4

    Dash 4 m, laying 1 Hoar on every enemy you pass within 1 m and dealing damage. On landing, enemies within 2 m carrying 3 or more Hoar are stunned for 0.5s as the frost sets. No return.

  • R

    Hoarfall

    100 mana · 110s cooldown · range 8

    Needs five layers of your own Hoar, which are spent. The wyrm bursts from your back and dives on a point within 8 m after a 0.8s warning: 150/250/350 (+0.8 bonus AD) magic damage in 3 m and a 0.75s knock-up, and every enemy hit is set straight to Brittle. For 4s afterwards your shatters deal double damage.
