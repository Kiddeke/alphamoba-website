The kit

  • P

    Hoarfrost

    Every hit you land or take lays a layer of Hoar: on the enemy a stacking slow, on you stacking damage reduction, five layers each. An enemy at five is Brittle, and your next ability shatters them for bonus damage. Your own five are what open the wyrm.

  • Q

    Flurry

    30 mana · 7s cooldown · range 2.2

    A three-step sword string on recasts: a lunge, a crosscut, an overhead. Each step lays Hoar. Paid once, and the cooldown starts when the string ends. As the wyrm: Rend, one sweep that cleaves everything in front of you.

  • W

    Frostbrand

    55 mana · 12s cooldown · range 2.4

    Plant the sword. Enemies nearby are dragged a step toward the blade and held as the ground rimes over under them. As the wyrm: Breath of Rime, a cone of frost that lays two layers of Hoar.

  • E

    Glide

    50 mana · 14s cooldown · range 4

    A short frost-trailing dash that lays Hoar on everyone you pass. Recast within 1.5s to dash back to where you started. As the wyrm: Stoop, a long dive that knocks up everything where you land.

  • R

    Wyrm of Rime

    100 mana · 120s cooldown · range 6

    Needs five layers of your own Hoar. Shed the armour and become the wyrm, sword in claw, for 8s and a second more for every layer of Hoar on the enemy champions around you. Your Hoar does not thaw while it lasts. Recast to drive the sword into the ground, shattering every layer in reach and ending the form.
