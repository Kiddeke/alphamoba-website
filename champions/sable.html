The kit

  • P

    Hollow Mark

    Ability hits mark enemy champions for 4s. Your basic attacks from behind a marked target deal 12% of their missing health as bonus damage, once per mark.

  • Q

    Fangfall

    60 energy · 9s cooldown

    Dash through the enemy under your cursor, striking them and landing behind them. Applies Hollow Mark.

  • W

    Fan of Knives

    40 energy · 7s cooldown · range 6

    Throw daggers in a cone for damage and a 30% slow. The centre dagger deals 50% bonus damage to champions.

  • E

    Veil

    80 energy · 20s cooldown

    Become untargetable and invisible for 1s, then gain 30% movement speed. You cannot act while Veiled.

  • R

    The Hollowing

    60s cooldown · range 8

    Mark the nearest enemy champion in range for 6s, gaining 40% movement speed and 20% bonus damage against them. Killing the marked target resets your cooldowns.
