The kit

  • P

    The Long Field

    The tip does the reaping. Your basic attacks and abilities deal +25% damage to enemies in the outer band of their reach (the last metre). Enemy champions below 35% health are Ripe: a sheaf glows over them for your team, and you move 12% faster toward them.

  • Q

    Reap

    8s cooldown · range 3.5

    Sweep a 180-degree, 3.5m arc: 70/105/140/175/210 (+0.9 bonus AD) physical damage. Champions caught in the tip band (2.5 to 3.5m) heal you for 15% of the damage dealt to them.

  • W

    Threshing

    14s cooldown

    For 3s your basic attacks swing a 120-degree arc at full reach, hitting everything in it (the tip band still applies), and you gain 20% attack speed.

  • E

    Hooked Blade

    13s cooldown · range 4.5

    Hook the scythe down a 4.5m line. The first enemy hit takes 50/80/110/140/170 (+0.6 bonus AD) physical damage, is dragged to 3m in front of you (right into the tip band) and is slowed 30% for 1s. If it hits nothing, you vault 3m forward on the haft.

  • R

    The Last Sheaf

    110s cooldown

    Wind up for 0.6s (the cut is drawn on the ground), then one 270-degree sweep, 4.5m: 150/225/300 (+1.0 bonus AD) physical damage. Enemy champions below 12/15/18% of max health are executed; in the tip band the threshold is a third higher. Killing a champion lets you recast within 5s, once per kill.
