The kit

  • P

    Roll Call

    You count as a minion: towers target you with minion priority and you take less damage from them. Every allied minion that dies near you leaves a Name, granting +1 health and +0.2 attack damage permanently. Cannons leave three.

  • Q

    Spearwork

    6s cooldown · range 2

    Stab with the broken spear, dealing double damage to enemies attacking an allied minion or tower.

  • W

    Shoulder to Shoulder, Lads

    18s cooldown · range 8.5

    Rally the nearby wave: allied minions take 50% less damage for 4s and follow you instead of the lane. Recast to send them back with brief haste.

  • E

    Not Today

    16s cooldown · range 7

    Swap places with an allied minion and leave them a shield worth 8% of your health.

  • R

    Field Promotion

    130s cooldown · range 8

    Plant the banner for 8s. Allied minions inside gain double health and damage and fight alongside you, allied towers inside fire faster, and each Name counts twice.
