TankMana

The kit

  • P

    Unmoved

    Sera cannot be displaced by enemies: hooks, pulls, knockbacks, knockups and carries do nothing to her. Stuns, roots, slows, silences and blinds all affect her normally, and terrain still blocks her.

  • Q

    Heaven's Palm

    30 mana · 8s cooldown · range 2.5

    An open-palm blow in a cone: physical damage, and enemies struck are pushed 150 units back.

  • W

    Wingwall

    50 mana · 18s cooldown

    Snap your wings wide for 2.5s: you become a moving wall that blocks all enemy projectiles that strike you, and move 20% slower while spread.

  • E

    Cast Out

    55 mana · 16s cooldown · range 2.5

    Seize an enemy within 250 units, hoist them, and throw them 400 units in the aimed direction: physical damage to the thrown target, and everyone at the landing point is slowed 30% for 1s.

  • R

    The Descent

    100 mana · 120s cooldown · range 6

    Ascend, and after 0.9s - the target ring drawn on the ground the entire time - crash down at a point up to 600 units away. Inner ring: heavy physical damage and a 1s knockup. Outer ring: 60% damage, and enemies are shoved 250 units outward from the crater.
