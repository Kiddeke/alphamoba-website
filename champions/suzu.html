The kit

  • P

    Reluctant Courage

    When an allied champion within 6m drops below 40% health (once per ally every 20s), Suzu steels herself: 25% move speed while running toward that ally for 2s, and her next Closed Thrust within 4s deals 25% more damage and slows everything it hits by 30% for 1s.

  • Q

    Closed Thrust

    35 mana · 8s cooldown · range 3.5

    Snap the umbrella shut and lunge 3.5m, stabbing everything in the line: 60/95/130/165/200 (+80% bonus AD) physical damage. Steeled by Reluctant Courage, it deals 25% more and slows by 30% for 1s.

  • W

    Rain Shelter

    40 mana · 14s cooldown · range 2.5

    Hold the umbrella straight up for 2s: every hit you take, from any direction, loses a flat 15/25/35/45/55 before armour and resist. Strong against many small hits, weak against one big one. You walk at 80% speed and cannot attack. When it closes (recast to close early) you shake the rain off: enemies within 2.5m are slowed by 25% for 1s.

  • E

    Catch the Wind

    40 mana · 16s cooldown

    Open the umbrella and ride a gust 425 units in any direction, backwards included. Landing shakes off the rain: enemies within 200 units are slowed 30% for 1s.

  • R

    Monsoon

    100 mana · 110s cooldown · range 4

    The umbrella opens to full size over you for 4s: a 4m dome that follows you. Allied champions under it take 25% less damage from enemies outside it. Enemies under it are slowed by 25% and pelted every 0.5s for 20/30/40 (+10% bonus AD) physical damage. When it ends, or on recast, the canopy snaps shut with a bat all round: 150/250/350 (+70% bonus AD) physical damage and a 1.5m push.
