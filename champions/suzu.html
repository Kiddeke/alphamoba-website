The kit

  • P

    Rain Check

    Damage the open umbrella blocks is banked on her palm sigil as Resolve, up to 25% of her maximum health. Her next Closed Thrust spends it all, adding 50% of the banked Resolve as bonus physical damage to the first champion hit. Resolve fades after 6s without blocking.

  • Q

    Closed Thrust

    35 mana · 8s cooldown · range 3.5

    Snap the umbrella shut and lunge 350 units, stabbing everything in the line: 60/95/130/165/200 (+80% bonus AD) physical damage, plus the Resolve bonus on the first champion. While the greatsword lasts this is Downpour: no lunge, a 270-degree, 350-unit cleave around her with the same damage and Resolve bonus.

  • W

    Open Umbrella

    40 mana · 14s cooldown · range 2.5

    Pop the canopy open toward the cursor for up to 2.5s (recast to close). She takes 40% less damage from the 120 degrees in front, walks at 75% speed and cannot attack; damage blocked becomes Resolve. Closing it bats the canopy shut: 40/65/90/115/140 (+40% bonus AD) physical damage, enemies in front pushed 150 units and slowed 30% for 1s.

  • E

    Catch the Wind

    40 mana · 16s cooldown

    Open the umbrella and ride a gust 425 units in any direction, backwards included. Landing shakes off the rain: enemies within 200 units are slowed 30% for 1s.

  • R

    The Change Finishes

    100 mana · 110s cooldown · range 4.5

    The umbrella becomes a full greatsword for 8s. On cast she leaps up to 450 units and slams down: 150/250/350 (+70% bonus AD) physical damage within 275 units and a 0.5s knock-up. While it lasts she gains 20/35/50 attack damage and 75 attack range, and Closed Thrust becomes Downpour. She cannot open the umbrella until the change fades.
