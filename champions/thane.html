The kit

  • P

    Hold the Line

    You take 4% less damage per enemy champion near you, to a maximum of 16%. Your attacks deal bonus damage to enemies facing away from you.

  • Q

    Banner Strike

    40 mana · 8s cooldown · range 3

    Sweep the banner-spear in an arc, damaging and slowing everything in front of you, with bonus damage to minions.

  • W

    Bulwark

    50 mana · 18s cooldown · range 8.5

    Raise the tower shield for 2.5s. Enemy projectiles that strike its front are stopped and you take 25% less damage from that side. You move slower while it is up. Recast to lower it.

  • E

    First In

    60 mana · 14s cooldown · range 6.5

    Charge in a line and stop on the first enemy champion, knocking them back and stunning them. Each ally you charge through extends the charge and grants you a shield.

  • R

    Plant the Banner

    100 mana · 130s cooldown · range 5

    Leap and slam the banner down, knocking up and damaging enemies nearby. The banner stands for 8s: allies inside take less damage and move faster, enemies inside are slowed. Three attacks destroy it.
