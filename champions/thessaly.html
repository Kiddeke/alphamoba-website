SupportMana

The kit

  • P

    Royal Jelly

    Her heals and shields leave allies Honeyed for 3s. An enemy champion who basic attacks a Honeyed ally gets stuck: slowed 15% for 1s.

  • Q

    Honey Drip

    60 mana · 8s cooldown · range 8

    Lob a glob of honey at a point. Allies in it are healed; enemies take magic damage and are slowed 30% for 1.5s. The puddle stays 2s, slowing enemies 15%.

  • W

    Amber Shell

    70 mana · 12s cooldown · range 7

    Shield an allied champion or herself for 2.5s. When it expires, whatever is left heals them for 50% of it.

  • E

    Sting

    50 mana · 14s cooldown · range 6.5

    Loose the swarm at an enemy champion: magic damage over 3s, and their healing and shielding are cut 40% for 3s. The bees return and heal her for 30% of the damage.

  • R

    Swarm Guard

    100 mana · 110s cooldown · range 6.5

    Send the hive to an allied champion or herself for 4s. Whenever an enemy champion damages them, a bee flies back at the attacker (at most once a second each) for magic damage and sticks them in honey, slowing 30% for 1s. When the swarm leaves, they are healed for 25% of the damage enemy champions dealt them meanwhile.
