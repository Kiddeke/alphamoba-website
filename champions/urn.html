The kit

  • P

    The Jar Takes Its Share

    The jar banks Ash from every hit you take and every blow you land. A hundred Ash hatches a Grub, to a maximum of three, and the count is visible to enemies. If you die, every grub you have placed dies with you.

  • Q

    Spadework

    35 mana · 8s cooldown · range 3

    Swing the grave-spade. Enemies carrying one of your brood return Ash to the jar.

  • W

    Set the Brood

    40 mana · 1s cooldown · range 5.5

    Spend a grub and throw it onto an enemy. It rides them and eats, to a maximum of two per host. It dies to a single hit from one of their allies, or when it leaves your sight.

  • E

    Split the Lid

    60 mana · 20s cooldown · range 6.5

    For 4s, the next three champions to damage you in melee have a grub knocked loose onto them, riding only briefly.

  • R

    Open the Jar

    100 mana · 120s cooldown · range 20

    Carry the jar open for 10s. Enemies who come near are latched, every grub of yours eats faster, and your grip reaches across the map.
