The kit

  • P

    The Dead Kneel

    Enemies you have slowed, stunned or taunted deal 15% less damage to you while it lasts.

  • Q

    Cleaver Chop

    40 mana · 8s cooldown · range 4

    After a 0.25s wind-up, bring the cleaver down along a 4m line: 70/110/150/190/230 (+5% max health) physical damage and a 35% slow for 1.5s. Double damage to minions.

  • W

    Collect the Tithe

    60 mana · 14s cooldown

    Wrench tribute from everyone kneeling to you: every enemy within 6m that currently Kneels (slowed, knocked up or taunted by you) takes 50/75/100/125/150 (+3% of your max health) magic damage. You then gain a shield of 50/70/90/110/130 (+4% max health), plus 25 (+2% max health) for each enemy champion you collected from (up to 3), for 3s. With nobody kneeling you get the base shield only.

  • E

    KNEEL.

    70 mana · 18s cooldown

    After a 0.3s wind-up, stamp the greatsword’s pommel: enemy champions within 3m are forced to their knees, stunned for 0.6s (minions 1s), and you gain 8 armour and magic resist for each champion downed (up to 24) for 4s.

  • R

    Throne of Skulls

    100 mana · 130s cooldown · range 6

    Raise a throne of skulls within 6m. It erupts after 0.5s, knocking up enemies within 2m for 0.75s, and stands for 6s over a 5m court. Enemies in the court are slowed 25%. While you are in the court you heal 1.5/2/2.5% of your max health per second for each enemy champion inside. Recast beside the throne to sit: rooted and unstoppable, you take 30% less damage, and every enemy champion in the court is taunted for 1s (once per cast). Recast to stand.
