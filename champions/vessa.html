The kit

  • P

    The First Taste Is Free

    Your abilities apply Desire to their target, to a maximum of 3 stacks. Your next attack spends every stack for bonus magic damage and heals you for part of it. Spending three stacks leaves the target walking toward you briefly.

  • Q

    Heartstring

    35 mana · 5s cooldown · range 6.5

    A whip-crack of heartstring in a line for damage and a stack of Desire. Landing it on a target that already has Desire refunds half the cooldown.

  • W

    Sweet Nothings

    60 mana · 14s cooldown · range 9

    Whisper to an enemy champion for 2.5s. While it holds, moving toward you grants them movement speed but increases the damage they take from you, and moving away slows them.

  • E

    Velvet Step

    40 mana · 12s cooldown

    Dash, leaving an afterimage behind for 3s. Your next attack strikes twice more, and a recast returns you to the afterimage.

  • R

    The Due

    100 mana · 110s cooldown · range 4.5

    Unleash a heart-shaped nova, dealing double damage to enemies below a third of their health. Kills refund half the cooldown.
