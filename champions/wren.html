The kit

  • P

    Full Draw

    Standing still for 1.25s nocks a Full Draw: your next basic attack reaches 1.5 further, deals 30% more damage and goes through its target into one more enemy behind it. Moving spends it; attacking does not.

  • Q

    Broadhead

    55 mana · 10s cooldown · range 12

    Press to start drawing (slowed by 30%), release or press again to loose: 7 on a tap, up to 12 after 1.5s of drawing, with up to 80% more damage. At full draw it pierces everything. Held for 3s, it looses on its own.

  • W

    Pin Down

    60 mana · 14s cooldown · range 8

    Shoot an arrow into the ground. After 0.4s enemies within 1.25 take physical damage and are pinned for 0.75s (longer with rank); twice as long beside a wall.

  • E

    Slip the String

    40 mana · 14s cooldown

    Hop in the aimed direction. Your next basic attack within 3s is a Full Draw.

  • R

    The Long Shot

    100 mana · 120s cooldown · range 25

    Root yourself and draw for 1.5s while a 25 line shows to everyone (recast to loose early for 60% damage). The first enemy champion hit takes physical damage and is driven back up to 4; if they strike a wall they are pinned to it and stunned.
