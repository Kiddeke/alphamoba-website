The kit

  • P

    Somewhere Safe

    Your buried Pips are hidden from enemies but chirp at anyone close and ripple the grass above them. Any reveal exposes a Pip permanently, after which a single hit destroys it and pays the destroyer. Six at a time, 90s each, and they trigger only on champions. You gain movement speed near your own Pips.

  • Q

    Nip

    30 mana · 7s cooldown · range 2.2

    Bite at the shins, dealing bonus damage to enemies who cannot see you.

  • W

    Stash

    40 mana · 14s cooldown · range 8.5

    Dig for 0.5s and bury a Pip. An enemy champion who steps near an armed one triggers it for damage, a slow, and a brief blind. Abilities, movement and vision are unaffected, and no Pip may blind the same target twice within 6s.

  • E

    Scoot

    50 mana · 13s cooldown

    A short hop. Landing near one of your Pips retrieves it, refunding the charge, and grants movement speed.

  • R

    Everybody Up!

    100 mana · 120s cooldown · range 7.2

    Every Pip rises and walks at the nearest enemy, revealed to both teams from the moment it stands, faster than a champion can walk, and destroyed by any single point of damage. Any Pip still standing after 5s buries itself again.
