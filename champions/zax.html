AssassinNo resource

The kit

  • P

    Four Eyes

    Every basic attack and ability that hits an enemy champion marks them for 8s. Marked champions are revealed to Zax alone, through fog, brush and invisibility. One eye of the mask lights per marked champion.

  • Q

    Drawn Edge

    7s cooldown · range 2.5

    A short slash on one enemy within 250 units: physical damage. Against a champion, Zax steals 20% of their move speed for 2s and heals for 30% of the damage dealt.

  • W

    Sword Vault

    14s cooldown · range 3.5

    Plant the katana and vault onto it, hopping up to 350 units, then slam down: magic damage in a 250-unit ring, 60% more within the inner 100 units. Untargetable for the 0.4s of the hop.

  • E

    Long Edge

    15s cooldown · range 4.5

    After a 0.35s wind-up the blade extends to twice its length and sweeps a 150-degree arc out to 450 units: physical damage, and enemies hit are rooted for 1s (+0.15s per rank).

  • R

    Undertow

    100s cooldown

    Sink into a pool of shadow for up to 5s: untargetable, invisible, unable to act, moving at 70% speed. Recast to surface early. On surfacing: +50% move speed for 4s, and for those 4s every basic attack takes 1s off all of your ability cooldowns.
